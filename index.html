Technical lead · Architecture to deployment

Turning regulatory documents into usable information.

Link to this project #

The challenge

Automate the extraction and classification of observations in FDA Form 483 documents for review by compliance teams.

The system

AWS document processing combines OCR, multi-label NLP, model training, orchestration, and a review interface. The published solution connects Textract, SageMaker, Step Functions, and serverless services.

As technical lead, I owned the solution from architecture through deployment and directed ML, frontend, and backend engineers. Computer vision · Edge + cloud

Loko AI

Forward deployed architect & hands-on ML lead

From roadside video to vehicle-speed alerts.

Link to this project #

The challenge

Estimate vehicle speed from fixed cameras and turn video into actionable overspeed events.

The system

Vehicle detection and tracking run at the edge. Per-camera calibration maps image coordinates to the road plane for speed estimation. Events flow to an AWS backend and React interface for review.

A field pilot across 3–4 cameras
Development, client handoff, and consultation during the client's subsequent rollout.

My responsibility

I worked as a forward deployed architect and technical lead for 2–3 engineers across software, DevOps, and frontend, alongside a project manager. I personally implemented the ML pipeline: detection, tracking, calibration, and speed estimation. After handoff, I supported the client's on-site team with rollout advice.
